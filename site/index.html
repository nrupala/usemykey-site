How it works

1. One master password

You pick one strong master password. It is the only key to your vault. Nobody can reset it for you — not even us — so there is no back door for anyone else either.

2. Everything is scrambled

Every password is encrypted before it is saved, each with its own key. Anyone who somehow gets your vault file sees only scrambled data.

3. Recovery words are your spare key

MyKey gives you 12 words to write on paper. Forget your master password, and the words open your vault. Keep the paper safe; it is a real key.

4. Your cloud carries the copies

Want the same vault on your phone and computer? Your own cloud folder (Drive, iCloud, OneDrive, Dropbox) carries the encrypted file. Your provider cannot read it.

What is inside

Sign-in codes built in

The 6-digit codes some sites ask for live right next to the password, encrypted the same way.

Strong passwords, made for you

MyKey writes passwords you could never invent — at least 16 characters — and shows a strength meter as you work.

Gentle change reminders

Each password remembers its birthday. After 180 days, MyKey suggests a change. You can change the period, or switch it off per password.

Backups that need two keys

A backup file opens with your recovery words and a separate backup code. One lost paper is not the whole story.

Fingerprint unlock on Android

Open the app with your fingerprint. Your master password stays the real key underneath, and always works.

Passkeys, kept in your vault

MyKey stores passkeys in the same vault and uses them to sign you in on Android. A passkey only ever answers the site it belongs to — a look-alike site gets nothing.

Fill logins in your browser

The free browser extension — Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers, plus Firefox — fills logins from your desktop vault. It holds no copy of your vault, talks only to the MyKey app on your own computer, and makes no internet requests at all.

Fill logins in other apps

On Android, MyKey can fill usernames and passwords into other apps and browsers. Nothing is filled until you unlock MyKey and tap the entry yourself.

A limited key for your AI helper

On desktop, you can give a program you trust — like an AI assistant — its own key that reads only the entries you pick. Never your sign-in codes. Every use is logged, and you can revoke it any time.

Copied passwords wipe themselves

Copy a password, paste it, and it clears itself a minute later by default — you can set 1 to 5 minutes. Locking the vault clears it at once.

Help inside the app

Plain-words help and common answers live inside the app, fully offline — including on the lock screen, where you are most likely to need them.

Looks the way you like

The phone and web apps follow your device's light or dark setting, font size, and motion choices — or pick a skin. Every skin is checked so text stays easy to read.
