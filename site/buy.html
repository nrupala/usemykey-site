How the licence works

After payment, we email you a licence key. Paste it into MyKey once, on any device. The key is kept inside your encrypted vault, so it travels with your vault to your other devices — one purchase covers them all, up to 20 connections in total.

The key is signed by us and checked inside the app. MyKey never phones home: there is no activation server, and the app keeps working offline, forever, whether or not this website exists.

If you already have more than 50 passwords in a vault, nothing is ever locked away from you. Your vault opens, reads, exports, and syncs as always — adding new passwords is what waits for a licence.
