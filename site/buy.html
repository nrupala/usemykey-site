How the licence works

After payment, we email you a licence key. Paste it into MyKey once, on any device. The key is kept inside your encrypted vault, so it travels with your vault to your other devices — one purchase covers them all, up to 20 connections in total.

The key is signed by us and checked inside the app. MyKey never phones home: there is no activation server, and the app keeps working offline, forever, whether or not this website exists.

If you already have more than 50 passwords in a vault, nothing is ever locked away from you. Your vault opens, reads, exports, and syncs as always — adding new passwords is what waits for a licence.

Refunds and returns
MyKey never phones home — by design, so your vault stays private. That also means we cannot see whether a licence key has been used, and we cannot switch a key off. So the deal is this, in plain words. Within 30 days of purchase, if you have not redeemed your key, you may return it for store credit — email info@aimlds.org from the address you paid with and tell us the key is unused; on return the licence is cancelled on our records and is never re-issued. After 30 days, a licence is treated as redeemed and the sale is final. You had the full free version — every safety feature, up to 50 passwords — to try before paying; the licence only lifts a count. Three things always stand, in any window: a duplicate charge is refunded, a key we failed to deliver and cannot recover is refunded, and anything the law requires is honoured. If a payment is refunded or charged back, the licence is void — we record that against the licence and treat the key as void, even though a key already in an app keeps working offline.
