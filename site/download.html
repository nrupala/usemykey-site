Web app (installable)

The same app runs in a browser and can be installed like a native app. It stores your vault on the device, in the browser, and works offline after the first load.

To install: open this page's app in Chrome or Edge on your phone or computer, then choose Install app (or Add to Home Screen) from the browser menu. On iPhone or iPad, open it in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen from the Share menu.

Moving from another password app?

Export a CSV file from Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or another password manager, then import it in MyKey. The file is read on your device only — it is never uploaded. See Support & FAQ for the steps.
