Recover my licence

New phone, new computer, fresh install — and the licence key is gone. That is recoverable. Here is exactly how it works.

The steps

1. Email us at info@aimlds.org from the email address you paid with, and say you lost your key. That is all we need — we do not ask for your date of birth, your payment date, or anything else. We hold the purchase details on our side already.

2. We find your licence in our records by that email address and check that it is active — a licence that was returned, refunded, or charged back is void and is not re-sent.

3. We re-send your licence key to that same email address — never to a different address. Control of that inbox is what proves the request is really yours, and it is why nothing else is needed.

4. Paste the key into MyKey on the new device. Done — the licence lives in your vault again.

Honest note: recovery is done by a person, not an instant lookup box, so allow a little time. And if you have lost access to the email address itself, write to us anyway with your Stripe receipt — those cases are reviewed by hand. Any page or form that asks you to type personal details into a web form to "look up" a MyKey licence is not ours — we do not run one.

While you're at it

When your key arrives, keep a copy somewhere safe that is not only on one device — a note in your MyKey vault itself works well.
